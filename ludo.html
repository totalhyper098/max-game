How to play Ludo

Goal — Get all 4 of your tokens from your yard, around the board, and into your home triangle before anyone else.

Starting — Roll a 6 to bring a token out of the yard onto your start square.

Moving — Tap the die to roll, then tap the token you want to move (if you have more than one legal option).

Capturing — Land exactly on an opponent's token to send it back to their yard. This can't happen on a star/safe cell.

Safe cells — Marked with a star. Tokens there can't be captured.

Extra turn — Rolling a 6, capturing a token, or getting a token home all earn you another roll. Three 6's in a row forfeits the turn.

Home stretch — You need the exact number to enter your home triangle — overshooting means that token can't move this turn.
