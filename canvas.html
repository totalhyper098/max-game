Carrom Rules

⚫ Black: 10 points

⚪ White: 20 points

👑 Queen: 50 points

🏆 Winning: First player to 180 points wins.

Extra shot: If you successfully pocket a coin, you get another turn.

Miss: If you don't pocket anything, the turn passes.

Foul: Pocketing the striker causes a 10-point penalty and the turn passes.

Queen: The queen must be covered by pocketing another coin in the same or following shot.

Aim: Pull the striker backward and release to shoot in the opposite direction.
