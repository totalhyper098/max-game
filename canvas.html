Rules

How to play Carrom

Scoring — Black = 10 pts · White = 20 pts · Queen = 50 pts. First to 180 points wins — this applies the same way in 2, 3, or 4 player matches.

Placing the striker — Drag the rail track around the edge of the board (outside the wood frame) to slide your striker along your baseline.

Shooting — Press and pull back on the striker itself, aim using the arrow, then release to shoot. Longer pull = more power.

Turns — Each player has 20 seconds per turn. Turns rotate in order after every shot.

Pockets — Get a coin into any corner pocket to score its points.

Foul — If your striker itself falls into a pocket, that turn earns no points and the striker returns to your baseline.

AI — Easy plays loose and mostly random. Medium aims for pocket-friendly coins with moderate accuracy. Hard calculates the actual angle needed to pot a coin and plays it with high precision.
